## 🎓 What Does It Do?

**Dynamic SLAM** tracks camera movement using only images - like GPS but with a camera!

### Real-World Applications:
- 🚗 **Self-driving cars** - Know where they are
- 🚁 **Drones** - Navigate and map areas
- 🤖 **Robots** - Move around without bumping into things
- 🥽 **AR/VR** - Track your head movement

### What You Get:
- **Trajectory Maps** - See the path the camera took
- **Feature Tracking** - Watch how the system follows points
- **Accuracy Graphs** - Compare with ground truth data

## 🚀 Quick Start Tutorial

### First Time? Do This:

1. **Launch the web interface:**
   - Double-click `launch_web.bat`
   - Browser opens automatically

2. **Read the welcome banner:**
   - Understand what Dynamic SLAM does
   - Click "Start Tutorial" (optional but fun!)

3. **Choose a sequence:**
   - Click the dropdown labeled "Select Video"
   - Pick any one (they're all driving footage!)

4. **Click "Quick Demo":**
   - Big button on the left
   - Processes 50 frames in ~1 minute

5. **Watch the magic:**
   - Progress bar fills up
   - Friendly messages guide you
   - Results appear automatically!

6. **Explore results:**
   - Click any image to enlarge
   - Download images you like
   - Try other sequences!
